**Project attribution**: Project done with a friend. <br>
**Source**: The project's files are available [here](https://github.com/RaduLucianR/vroom). <br>
**Short description**: RC car made from scratch with 3D printed parts. <br>
**Technologies**: ROS, C++, Raspberry Pi <br>
**What I did**: I worked on the car's assembly, designed the ROS2 architecture and deployed it on a Raspberry Pi to control the car's throttle and steering using GPIO and PWM.
